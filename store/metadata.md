## Description
Loftmeter turns your iPhone into a precision attitude-telemetry instrument for
motorcycles. Mount your phone, calibrate once, and Loftmeter measures your
pitch angle in real time — reading true wheelie angle, speed, and how long you
held it.

WHAT IT MEASURES
• Real-time pitch (wheelie) angle from the phone's motion sensors
• Speed and distance from GPS
• Per-run peak angle, top speed, and hold duration
• A reviewable trace of every run, saved on your device

HOW IT WORKS
• Calibrate once at the start of a session — hold the bike steady and Loftmeter
  measures the sensor bias, then you swipe to align it to your bike.
• A live meter shows your angle as you ride, with an optional audio cue.
• Every run is scored and saved so you can review your line afterward.

PRIVACY
Your rides never leave your phone. No account, no tracking, no ads. Location
and motion data are used only to compute your run metrics, stored on device,
and deletable any time.

SAFETY FIRST
Loftmeter is built to keep you in control, not to push you further. There is no
leaderboard and no score to chase — instead, an audio cue warns you when your
angle gets too high so you can ease off before you lose control. Ride only where
it is legal and safe to do so — closed courses or private property — wear
appropriate gear, and obey all local laws. You are solely responsible for how
you ride.
