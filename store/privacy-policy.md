# Loftmeter — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-09_

Loftmeter ("the app") is a motorcycle attitude-telemetry instrument. This policy
explains what the app accesses and what it does — and does not — do with it.

## Summary

Loftmeter stores your ride data **only on your device**. It does not have
accounts, does not upload your rides to any server, and does not use analytics,
advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Information the app accesses

- **Location (GPS).** Used to measure speed and distance during a run, including
  while the app runs in the background during a ride. Location is processed on
  your device to compute run metrics and is stored on your device with the run.
  It is **not** transmitted off the device by the app.
- **Motion & orientation sensors** (gyroscope / accelerometer via Core Motion).
  Used to measure pitch angle (wheelie angle) and to calibrate. Processed and
  stored on device only.

## Information the app does NOT collect

- No name, email, account, or contact information.
- No advertising identifier; no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
- No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit personal data.
- No sale or sharing of personal information.

## Data storage and retention

All runs, calibration data, and diagnostic logs are stored locally on your
device. You can delete individual runs, or all runs, from within the app
(Past Runs → settings → Delete All). Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Children

Loftmeter is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.

## Your controls

- Grant or revoke Location and Motion permissions any time in iOS Settings.
- Delete your data in-app or by uninstalling.
