WHAT THE APP IS
Loftmeter is an attitude-telemetry instrument for motorcycles. It uses the
iPhone's motion sensors to measure pitch angle and GPS for speed/distance, and
saves a reviewable record of each run on the device.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT A MOTORCYCLE
The app works from any motion. To exercise it on a desk:
1. Launch the app; on first launch accept the safety disclaimer.
2. Hold the phone still to complete calibration, then swipe to align.
3. Tilt the phone up/down to see the live pitch angle move on the meter.
4. Stop the run; open Past Runs to see the saved run and its trace.
Grant Motion and Location permissions when prompted.

PERMISSIONS
- Location (When In Use / background while a run is active): computes speed and
  distance during a ride. Declared with NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription and
  NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription; background mode "location" is
  used only while a run is recording.
- Motion: measures pitch angle. NSMotionUsageDescription.
- Audio background mode: keeps the optional in-run audio cue alive when the
  screen locks during a ride.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. All ride data is
stored on-device and user-deletable.

SAFETY POSITION (re: guidelines 1.1.6 / 1.4.1)
Loftmeter is a safety and measurement instrument, not a stunt-encouragement
app. Specifically:
- There is NO leaderboard, NO ranking against other users, and NO gamified
  incentive to "go bigger." The app does not reward larger or riskier wheelies.
- The audio cue is a WARNING: it alerts the rider when their pitch angle gets
  too high, so they can back off before losing control. Its purpose is to
  PREVENT the rider from going past a safe angle, not to push them further.
- The app measures and records attitude for a rider's own review; it does not
  instruct, coach, or reward illegal riding.
A first-launch disclaimer requires the user to acknowledge riding only where
legal and safe (closed course / private property), wearing gear, and obeying
local law, and that they assume all risk.
