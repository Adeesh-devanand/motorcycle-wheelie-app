## Common questions

**How do I calibrate?**
At the start of a session, hold the bike steady (engine off is easiest) until
the calibration progress completes, then swipe along the chassis to align the
sensor to your bike.

**Why does the angle look wrong?**
Re-calibrate with the phone already mounted in its riding orientation. The first
calibration of a session sets the reference for that session.

**Does Loftmeter upload my rides?**
No. All runs are stored on your device only. Delete them any time in
Past Runs → settings → Delete All.

**What permissions does it need, and why?**
- Location (GPS): to measure speed and distance during a run.
- Motion & Fitness: to measure pitch angle from the phone's motion sensors.
